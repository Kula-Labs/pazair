# Kula Labs teams

Teams that work on PazAIr. Each team has a folder with a charter (who, why, what they owe), a roster,
and the artefacts they produce. Hired by the CEO; every charter is signed off here.

| Team | Lead | Owes the company |
|---|---|---|
| [US Market & Product Catalog](toni-strake/CHARTER.md) | Toni Strake | The daily Product Development Catalog for new PazAIr listings, built from Vision and Echo telemetry |

## Inputs every team can count on

- **Vision** and **Echo** send request telemetry several times a day: what agents asked the market
  (`ask_market` goals), what they bought, what they could not find (`/v1/wishes`), from where, in which language.
- The [Word Pass specification](../SPEC.md): what a listing must promise and what a delivery must prove.
- The [Kodex](../README.md#the-kodex): every listing a team proposes has to be sellable under it.
