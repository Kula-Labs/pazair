# Governance

The rules are in [SPEC.md section 11](SPEC.md#11-governance). In short:

- **Free to implement.** No permission, fee, membership or contract to issue, show or check a Word Pass. MIT licensed;
  Kula Labs will not assert any patent against an implementation.
- **Earned, never bought.** Issuers may charge for their own services. No one sells a record, a badge, a rank, a seal
  or anyone's data.
- **No privileged issuer.** PazAIr is the first issuer, not a special one.
- **Nothing changes in silence.** Every change is an issue or pull request here; fields are only added; a change that
  is not a fix against abuse or a security problem is announced at least 14 days before issuers apply it.
- **A neutral home.** An issuer whose implementation reproduces the test vectors may ask for a co-editor seat. With
  three issuers not controlled by Kula Labs, the specification goes to a W3C Community Group, then an IETF draft.

## Proposing a change

Open an issue: what breaks or is missing today, the smallest change that fixes it, and a test vector that shows it.
Breaking a pass, a receipt or the chain is welcome: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
